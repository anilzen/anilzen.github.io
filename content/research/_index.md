My research concerns the geometry and computation of waves on infinite domains. I develop **hyperboloidal methods** that bring null infinity onto a finite computational grid, connecting questions in general relativity with numerical analysis and scattering theory.

Recent results include gravitational-wave extraction from numerical relativity simulations, the study of late-time wave tails, and finite element methods for scattering. I also work on swarm-based optimization for non-convex problems. The pages below introduce each area and link to its publications and software.

See the [full publication list](/publication/) for journal references and preprints, and the [NSF Award page](/nsf-award-2309084/) for the research and educational activities supported by that grant.
