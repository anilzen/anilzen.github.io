abstract: >-
  Time functions with asymptotically hyperbolic geometry play an increasingly important role in many
  areas of relativity, from computing black-hole perturbations to analyzing wave equations. Despite
  their significance, many of their properties remain underexplored. In this expository article, I
  discuss hyperbolic time functions by considering the hyperbola as the relativistic analog of a
  circle in two-dimensional Minkowski space and argue that suitably defined hyperboloidal
  coordinates are as natural in Lorentzian manifolds as spherical coordinates are in Riemannian
  manifolds.
